# Decisions

Append-only log of choices made where DESIGN.md was ambiguous.


- [map] Start mine "gap of 3" = Chebyshev gap in empty tiles between footprints: max(0, b.x-(a.x+a.s), a.x-(b.x+b.s), same for y) === 3 (`footprintGap` in mapgen.js). The sim's "Town Center ≥ 3 tiles from any mine" rule should use the same metric so start TCs are valid placements.
- [map] Maps are rotationally symmetric: 180° for 1–2 players, 90° for 3–4 (every start gets the identical base, wood line and expansions). With 3 players the unused bottom-left start (and with 1 player the bottom-right) becomes a rich expansion: EXPANSION_MINE_AMOUNT mine + level-3 camp where the town center would be. 2 players: 1 flank natural (level 2) + 1 contested corner expansion (level 3) each; 4 players: 1 edge expansion each, plus an inner natural (level 2) on medium.
- [map] `camps[i] = { x, y, level }`: x, y is the camp center in world tile units (a tile center, or exactly the map center for a center camp); ≥ 2.6 tiles of open ground around it. Every expansion mine has a level 2–3 camp 4–6 tiles from the mine center. `mines`: start mines first (player order), then expansions.
- [map] `heights[vy * (width + 1) + vx]` is the vertex at world corner (vx, vy). Vertices touching only water = -0.6, water-edge vertices = -0.35, land ≥ -0.05 rising to hills over ~4 tiles from the shore, max 0.8 (rock tiles are bumped up). A water plane at about -0.2 lines up with the tile edges.
- [map] Decor stays 11.5+ tiles from start town centers, 4.2 from mines and 2.4 from camps; elsewhere it can sit where a player later builds, so the renderer should hide (or ignore) decor under building footprints.
- [map] Starts, mine rings and camps are 4-connected on foot, and every start's front yard, expansion clearing and camp is also joined by 3-wide routes (every tile's 3×3 block walkable) so titans can reach everything. mapData also carries a `meta` object ({ symmetry, center, lakes, river, attempt }) for debugging; it is not part of mapHash.
- [map] `seed` may be a number or a string; it is hashed via its string form, so 1 and '1' give the same map. mapSize also accepts an even number (64–256) for tests.
- [sim] Spell (ability) damage ignores armor and does × SPELL_VS_BUILDING to buildings; the WC3 armor formula only applies to attacks. Splash from attacks is 50% and goes through armor.
- [sim] Casts: a unit walks into range first (point blink/dash just clamp to max range), then winds up `castTime`. If it can't get into range the error is 'Target out of range.'. Instant self-casts (no target, castTime 0) run right away and don't interrupt the current order; a non-queued cast resumes the attack / attackMove / hold it interrupted.
- [sim] Only one tier_up per player at a time. Train/tier-up at a Town Center that is growing → 'Your settlement is already growing.' (new error text; was 'The Town Center is growing.'). Player tier is never below 1 (it is the highest tier among the player's finished Town Centers, else 1).
- [sim] Build command: the closest selected worker pays and places the site; the other selected workers get help-build orders. Unplaced paid builds are refunded in full when the order is replaced or the site is blocked; cancelling a placed site refunds 75%.
- [sim] God XP: "opposing side" = the killer's team. Living gods of that team within XP_RANGE of the dead unit split xpValue evenly (rounded). Neutral creep kills give XP the same way. Creep bounty also emits `{ type: 'bounty', player, amount, x, y }`.
- [sim] Fields deal/heal on creation, then every 0.5 s while `rem > 0`. DoT statuses tick every 0.5 s. Auras are recomputed every 10 ticks and don't stack (max positive / min negative per stat).
- [sim] Creeps: level-1 camp = 2–3 tier-1 creatures; level 2 = tier-2 + 2 tier-1; level 3 = tier-3 + 2 tier-2 (random across all pantheons, world.rng). They aggro within CREEP_AGGRO_RANGE or when hit, the whole camp joins, and they leash at CREEP_LEASH_RANGE from the camp center (or when the target is past leash+2), then walk home and heal to full.
- [sim] Rally points: on an ally unit → follow, on an enemy → attackMove, on a mine/tree → workers gather, otherwise move. Units with radius ≥ 0.75 (titans, big creatures) path on a 3×3-clearance grid, falling back to the plain grid if no route.
- [sim] Victory is only checked when the game started with ≥ 2 teams (solo sandbox never ends on its own). If every remaining player is defeated on the same tick, gameOver = { winningTeam: null } (draw). Surrender also destroys the surrendering player's buildings.
- [sim] Snapshot conventions: units always carry c / cd / fx / sh; buildings always carry q / rp; a unit chasing a target shows st 'move'; an unfinished building shows st 'build' and bp stays ≤ 0.99 until complete. Internally `player.fallen[type] = { level, xp }`; the snapshot exposes `fallen[type] = level`. Effect events also carry `src`, `ability` and (when delayed) `delay`; chain effects carry `points`, blink/dash carry `fx, fy`.
- [content A] Ability `range` is center-to-target-edge but attack range is edge-to-edge, so an autocast ability whose range equals the attack range never fires (sim autocasts don't chase). Autocast creature abilities use range ≥ attack range + own radius + ~0.5 (e.g. Naga Venom Spit 5.5 vs attack 4.5, Pegasus Thunder Bearer 5 vs 3.5). Other content designers: same pitfall.
- [content A] Ally-shield autocasts (Valkyrie) use `ai.use: 'heal'` so the sim's autocast picks the most wounded ally in range (with 'buff' it would always shield itself). Minions use `buildTime: 0` (never produced). Ultimates that are pure buffs/heals use target 'none' centred on the caster.
- [content A] Visual approximations within the schema: Set = jackal head + tall_crown (no Set-animal head); Cyclops = giant + `third_eye` (reads as the single eye); Sphinx = quadruped + human head + nemes; Pegasus = quadruped + horse head + wings; Valkyrie and Garuda = humanoid + wings with air: true; Airavata = elephant with `rider` (Indra) and `heads: 3`; Cerberus = quadruped with `heads: 3`. Renderers may ignore `heads` for non-hydra bodies.
- [content A] Balance (sim duels, scratch script): titans' auto-attack duels end within ±5% hp of each other; T1/T2 same-cost brawls end with the winner at 5–25% hp. Garuda (T3 air) cannot be hit by melee or by the Cyclops' rocks, so it sits at the low end of T3 hp/dps (1000 hp, 28 dps).
- [content B] Ids drop apostrophes/diacritics: Chang'e → `chinese_chang_e` (apostrophe as a word break), Nüwa → `chinese_nuwa`, Myōbu Fox → `japanese_myobu_fox`, Yamata no Orochi → `japanese_yamata_no_orochi`; display names keep them.
- [content B] Dash moves the caster over the following ticks but its other effects resolve at cast time, so "stun where he lands" (Guan Yu's Red Hare Charge) is `at: 'self'` with `delay: 0.45` (8 tiles ÷ 20 tiles/s + margin). Blink moves the caster instantly, so `at: 'self'` effects after a blink (Tezcatlipoca's Night Wind, Sun Wukong's Somersault Cloud) land at the destination with no delay. Both checked in the sim.
- [content B] A pull centred on the target unit does nothing (zero distance), so the Ahuizotl's single-target grab is a pull `at: 'self'`, radius 3.5, `maxTargets: 1` (nearest enemy), then damage + stun on the target. Tengu's point-target autocast uses range 6.5 vs attack range 4.5 (same pitfall content A logged).
- [content B] Passives carry optional `name` and `description` fields for HUD tooltips (the validator ignores them). Roles: Japanese Amaterasu support / Susanoo warrior / Raijin nuker / Inari summoner; Chinese Sun Wukong trickster / Nüwa support-summoner / Guan Yu warrior / Chang'e nuker; Aztec Quetzalcoatl support / Huitzilopochtli warrior / Tezcatlipoca trickster / Tlaloc nuker-summoner.
- [content B] Balance (sim duels vs the _placeholder budget-centre units): Japanese/Chinese/Aztec titans within ±7% of each other; T1 within ±17%; T2 air mirror (Tengu vs Fenghuang) within ±11%; T3 Oni vs Xiuhcoatl even. Air beats melee-only creatures outright by design (Aztec has no air creature; its answer is the ranged Xiuhcoatl, gods and archers).
- [content C] Ids: The Dagda → `celtic_the_dagda`, The Morrígan → `celtic_the_morrigan` (the article is part of the name), Cù Sìth → `celtic_cu_sith`, Anzû → `mesopotamian_anzu`, Mušḫuššu → `mesopotamian_mushussu`; display names keep diacritics. Roles: Celtic Dagda tank-healer / Morrígan trickster-summoner / Lugh nuker / Brigid healer; Mesopotamian Marduk nuker / Ishtar mobility warrior / Enki healer-summoner / Nergal tank; West African Shango nuker / Oya support-control / Ogun tank / Anansi trickster-summoner.
- [content C] DESIGN 4.1 "melee creatures cannot hit air" is applied literally to the flying melee Sasabonsam (`canHitAir: false`); its autocast Hook-Foot Snatch (spells ignore canHitAir, range 4.5) is its only answer to flyers. Melee summoned flyers (Battle Crow) keep `canHitAir: true` like the Norse Raven. Some visuals set the optional `visual.colors.hair` (supported by unit-parts.js), e.g. Brigid red, Banshee silver.
- [content C] Two-pass line (Lugh's Spear of Assal): a second identical `line` effect with `delay: 0.5` re-sweeps the cast-time line (origin and direction are frozen at cast), i.e. the spear "returns"; only the first pass emits a VFX event.
- [content C] Balance (sim auto-attack duels vs every pantheon's same-tier unit, 3 seeds × both sides): T1 avg −0.04..+0.02; T2 air-vs-air −0.04 (Banshee) / ±0.05 (Anzû), Sasabonsam loses to ranged flyers by design but wins vs ground melee; T3 ground matchups avg −0.01..+0.06 (all lose to T3 flyers like everyone's melee T3); titans within ±6% of the field; god auto-attack spreads match other pantheons (tanks +0.2..+0.27, supports −0.17). onHitSlow also slows attack speed, so titan/T3 onHitSlow is capped at 15–20%.
- [unit models] Triangle budgets: mortals ≤ 500, gods ≤ 1200, titans ≤ 2000 (DESIGN); creatures and minions (no DESIGN number) ≤ 1200. The ≤ 8 meshes per instance count includes the aura disc, the flyer blob shadow and worker cargo. Extra heads of multi-headed beasts (Cerberus, Airavata) are built at low detail and only the centre head wears headgear. `tests/render/unit-models.test.js` enforces all of this for every unit type.
- [unit models] Team color placement (reads from the 56° camera): sash + shoulder pads on gods/soldiers/creature riders, the hood itself on hooded mortals (worshipper/archer/priest), belt on workers and spirits, cape when `cape` is listed, helmet crest, shield face; saddle cloth + collar on quadrupeds (team bands instead from scale 2.6 up), bands on serpents/dragons/hydras/nagas, caparison on elephants, roof + flag on the battering ram, strap on carried wood.
- [unit models] `visual.heads` is honoured on humanoids (up to 3), quadrupeds (up to 5), elephants (3), winged dragons (3) and hydras (any; Orochi shows 8). Grounded units with `wings` fold them along the back while standing and spread/flap when moving, attacking, casting or flying. Headless riders (`head: 'none'` + lantern/orb/torch offhand, i.e. the Dullahan) hold the offhand aloft.
- [unit models] Hair color = `visual.colors.hair` when set, else a small built-in table for famous figures (Zeus white, Thor red …), else a dark brown derived from the skin. Animal heads use `skin`; manes/backs use `primary` when it differs from skin.
- [unit models] `model.height` is the top of the model in its rest pose, including the 2.2 hover of air units (for health bars / selection). Auras are a ground glow of radius ≈ 0.8 × (0.55 + 0.35 × scale) tiles so titans do not drown their neighbours. Death: topple by ~0.6 s, lie still, sink out of sight by ~1.55 s; if `attackPhase` is never supplied the model loops its own swing on the unit's attack cooldown.
- [buildings] Building/mine models put their origin at the footprint's highest terrain vertex (`footprintHeight(map, tx, ty, size)` in `src/render/height.js`); a stone foundation reaches 0.65 below it so slopes never show a gap. Every model stays inside size/2 − 0.1 of its center (a safety net scales anything that would overhang), fronts face +Z (south, toward the camera), ≤ 3000 triangles and ≤ 6 meshes (base / body / team / glow, + vortex or scaffold) for every pantheon and every DESIGN 4.7 style combination; `tests/render/building-models.test.js` enforces it.
- [buildings] Style values drive one composition per roof for temples and the town center (e.g. longhouse = Norse hall with dragon gables, stepped + mudbrick = ziggurat, stepped + other walls = Mesoamerican twin-shrine pyramid, dome = Sudano-Sahelian mosque, thatch_cone = roundhouse in a stone ring); walls and ornaments vary materials and props on top. Town center tiers: 1 = Village (hall + yard props), 2 = Town (low wall, two gate towers), 3 = City (crenellated walls, four towers, two-storey palace). Team color: roof ridge/eave bands, flags, banners, shields, gate plates. Construction = clip shader rising with bp + a scaffold, fires/vortex hidden until complete.
- [props] Forest: 1–2 trees per FOREST tile (stable per seed), pine/birch in cool noise patches, broadleaf elsewhere, palms within 2 tiles of sand/water; one InstancedMesh per 32×32 chunk × variant. `fell(tx, ty)` zeroes that tile's instances and adds a stump (mapData is never modified); `isStanding(tx, ty)` and `count` are extras. Decor gets `hideInRect(x0, y0, x1, y1)` so buildings can clear rocks/bushes under their footprint.
- [vfx] `VFXSystem(scene, getHeight, options?)` takes an optional third argument `{ quality: 'low'|'medium'|'high', maxParticles }` (particle cap 1600 / 3000 / 4000; quality also scales emission counts, `setQuality(q)` changes it live). Under load an emission budget scales new particles down once the pool is >55 % full (floor 8 %), so the cap is never exceeded and old particles are never popped. Everything is pooled (particles, decals, ribbons, flares, pillars, domes, instanced projectile meshes, effect records); `update(dt)` allocates nothing and finished effects release every resource. `clear()` / `stats()` / `setFocus(x, y)` / `addShake(amount, x, y)` are extras.
- [vfx] `spawnEffect` also accepts `delay` (seconds until impact, as the sim's effect event already carries) and `follow(out) → bool`. With a delay the shape shows a pulsing telegraph ring and starts when it ends; meteor uses the delay as its fall time and storm as the time to its first strike. `follow` keeps shield / buff / heal visuals on a moving unit (write its current {x, y} into `out`; return false when it is gone and the visual finishes in place). Without `follow`, shield lasts `duration` clamped to 1–1.8 s and buff/heal are ~1.1 s bursts, so a long-buff ability does not leave a dome hanging where the unit used to stand.
- [vfx] `spawnProjectile` also accepts `track(out) → bool` for homing: write the target's current {x, y[, h]} so the shot curves onto a moving target; return false when the target is gone and the shot finishes on its last known point. `flight` defaults to distance / PROJECTILE_SPEED. Unknown kinds draw as an orb, unknown elements use the arcane palette, NaN heights are treated as 0.
- [vfx] Shapes that need an origin (beam, cone, wave, dash, projectile, blink) use `fx, fy` when given; without one, beam comes down from the sky, cone becomes a nova, wave becomes an expanding ring, dash becomes a flash at the destination, blink shows only the arrival, projectile drops an orb from the sky. Chain arcs through `points`, starting from fx, fy when given. quake / meteor / strike / summon of scale ≥ 2.8 add camera shake: `vfx.shake` is an amplitude in world units (0..1, exponential decay, attenuated by distance from `setFocus`).
- [vfx] Look: glows use premultiplied "additive with a little occlusion" blending (src + dst·(1 − k·coverage), k ≈ 0.2–0.45) instead of pure additive, so element hues stay saturated over bright grass rather than washing out to white; shadow / death / chaos / poison effects get a tinted normal-blended "gloom" underlay. The particle atlas is a CanvasTexture (radial-gradient glow + procedural star / smoke / spark / ring / shard / plus / flame cells); in Node (tests) it falls back to an equivalent DataTexture.
- [network] Wire framing (src/net/protocol.js): frames are strings sent with PeerJS `serialization: 'raw'` (PeerJS's 'json' mode refuses messages ≥ 16300 bytes). A JSON message longer than 16000 characters is split into ordered `~seq,i,n|…` chunks. Idle connections send a heartbeat 'h' every 2 s, close() sends a goodbye 'b', and a connection silent for 15 s closes itself (reason 'timeout'), so closed tabs (BroadcastChannel) and dead WebRTC links are noticed even without a close event. The heartbeat timers are worker-driven like the ticker, so background tabs keep them.
- [network] Connections: `onMessage` / `onClose` return an unsubscribe function. Messages that arrive while nobody listens are buffered and delivered in order to the next listener, which is how the lobby hands its connections to the game sessions (a client may build its ClientSession some time after `start` without losing deltas). Extras: `conn.open`, `conn.closeReason`, `conn.stats`. LobbyHost.create / LobbyClient.join also accept `transport: 'loopback'` (in-memory rooms for Node tests). readNetParams also reads `?transport=local` (or `?net=local`).
- [network] Lobby: the host is always slot 0, and a joining human takes the first 'open' slot. Presets move connected humans to the first slots and fill the rest. Online, 1v1 and coop keep one open slot for a friend (offline that slot becomes an AI); 2v2 and ffa fill the rest with AI Normal, and the host can turn any AI slot back to open. `applyPreset` returns false when the connected players don't fit. Colors stay unique: a client's setMine color that is already taken is ignored, and when the host takes an AI's color the two swap. Duplicate names get " (2)". Extras: `startProblem()` (reason text or null), `kick(i)`, `close()`, LobbyClient `leave()`. Chat lines (lobby and game) are `{ from: slot or player index, -1 for system, name, text, system }`.
- [network] Start: the seed is a random 31-bit integer. 'random' pantheons are resolved with RNG(hashSeed(`${seed}:pantheons`)), preferring pantheons nobody else plays. Open and closed slots are skipped, so a player's index can differ from its slot index. `setup.mapHash` = mapHash(generateMap(…)). After start the LobbyHost turns late joiners away ('The game has already started.') and destroys its PeerJS peer once every game connection has closed.
- [network] HostSession: one snapshot per ticker wake (a wake runs up to 5 catch-up ticks), sent as a delta to each client and encoded once per distinct base. `full` carries `felledAll` = trees felled before that snapshot (its own come in `felled`). A disconnected human gets createAI(world, i, 'normal') unless already defeated or the game is over; the system line reads "<name> has left the game. An AI (Normal) takes over.". `stop(reason)` sends `end` ('game-over' after a victory, else 'host-quit') and does not fire the host's own onEnd. Extras: `step(n)` (manual ticks), `peerPings` (Map of client-reported RTTs; the host's own `pingMs` is 0), `world`, `isHost`.
- [network] ClientSession listens from construction; start() only starts the 2 s pings. Extras: `ready` (a Promise) / `onReady(cb)` resolve on the first full snapshot, plus `requestResync()`, `resyncs`, `mapMismatch`, `pingMs` (null until the first pong) and `isHost: false`. onEnd reasons: 'host-left' (link lost), 'host-quit', 'game-over', or a kick reason. `stop()` leaves the game, and the host's AI takes over.
- [network] ViewState keeps one persistent view object per entity id. It assigns each snapshot's fields into that object and deletes optional fields that have gone, and never mutates snapshot objects, so the delta codec's bases stay intact. Allied vision is shared by team. My team's `reveals` entries are sight sources (this answers the [sim] / [content] requests). Defeated players, spectators (myIndex −1) and everyone after game over see the whole map. Helpers: `isEntityVisible(e)` (units: their tile is visible; buildings: any footprint tile visible; mines: explored), `relation(owner)` → 'own' | 'ally' | 'enemy' | 'neutral', `isAlly`, `isEnemy`, `teamOf`. `lastSeen` also holds the enemy buildings visible right now (refreshed every snapshot), and drops one once its footprint is seen without it. A tree known only from `felled` / `felledAll` gets a synthetic `treeFelled` event (`sync: true` for resync ones), so the renderer only needs to listen to the event. Extras: `vs.felledAll`, `vs.mapVersion` (bumped per felled tree), `vs.renderTime`.
- [network] Interpolation clock: the offset between sim time and local time tracks the lowest latency seen. It drops at once, creeps up by at most 2 ms per snapshot, and resets after a jump of more than 1 s. Units render at that offset minus interpDelayMs. A move of more than 4 tiles between two samples (a blink) jumps instead of sliding. Delta format: see the header of src/net/delta.js. Equality follows JSON (=== on primitives, so −0 equals 0); keys whose value is undefined are dropped, as JSON does.
- [network] `src/net/fake-world.js` (FakeWorld + makeFakeMap) is a seeded stand-in World (issueCommand / tick / getSnapshot / mapData) used by tests/net and tools/net-test.html; the game never loads it. Browser check: `node tools/net-test.mjs [--shots DIR]` starts http-server :8104 and a PeerJS server on 127.0.0.1:9104 (bound to IPv4 explicitly because this machine has no IPv6). It then plays lobby + game over WebRTC (FakeWorld with a 130 KB full snapshot, and the real World), and over BroadcastChannel, and compares the client's snapshot hash with the host's at every tick.
- [network] Polish: the protocol version is `GAME_VERSION+<8 hex digits>` (src/net/wire-version.js, which replaces `PROTOCOL_VERSION` in protocol.js): an FNV-1a fingerprint of `MSG`, `EVENT_SHAPES`, the command specs and the sorted unit / building / ability / pantheon ids. It cannot be forgotten at a wire change, and a mismatch is reported by the existing "Version mismatch" kick (the text shows both full strings). Backpressure is per client and loss-free: a delta is skipped while `conn.buffered` (the RTCDataChannel's `bufferedAmount`) exceeds 128 KB, and the catch-up delta carries the skipped snapshots' events and felled trees (`c.held`); more than 2000 held items falls back to a full snapshot once the queue drains. `ClientSession.stalledMs` is the connection's `silentMs` (it ignores local stalls). Preset highlight: only a slot's kind (human and open count as the same), team or AI difficulty clears `state.preset`. Duplicate names in the GameSetup get " 2", " 3"; lobby slots are unchanged. `LobbyHost.close()` after the start closes the endpoint (and any open game link) after 1 s; `LobbyClient.leave()` after the start closes the link.
- [menu/audio] Menu API (src/ui/menu.js): `createMenu({ root, onStart })` builds the whole overlay in `#menu-root` (main.js does this once); `showMenu(screen = 'title', { keepLobby })` / `hideMenu()` are the module-level controls. `onStart(launch)` gets `{ role: 'host', setup, myIndex, conns: Map, lobby }` for host and single player, or `{ role: 'client', setup, myIndex, conn, lobby }` for a client. The menu keeps the launched lobby and closes it the next time `showMenu()` runs unless `keepLobby: true`, so calling showMenu after a match also frees the PeerJS room. Kicked clients return to the Join screen with the reason shown.
- [menu/audio] "Play vs AI" = LobbyHost transport 'none' + the coop preset, trimmed to one human (team 1) vs one AI Normal (team 2) on a small map, other slots closed; the player can change everything in the lobby before Start. Host-only controls: the kind dropdown (Open is offered only online; switching a connected human to Open calls `lobby.kick`), teams, presets, map size. Start is disabled with the text of `startProblem()` (falling back to `canStart()`). URL params: `?join=CODE` opens Join with the code filled (removed from the URL after the attempt), `?transport=local|peer` picks the online transport, `?mockLobby=1` uses src/ui/menu-mock.js instead of src/net/lobby.js (screenshots and UI work without networking). Invite links keep transport/peer*/turn* params.
- [menu/audio] Settings (src/settings.js): stored under 'mythicwar-settings', every storage access in try/catch (falls back to memory). Values are sanitized on load and save: name trimmed, control characters removed, max 20 chars, empty → generated 'Player' + 3 digits (the generated name is saved so it stays stable); volume clamped 0..1; quality must be low/medium/high/ultra; edgeScroll/showAllBars booleans. `getSettings()` returns a frozen object; `onSettingsChange(cb)` calls `cb(next, changedKeys)` and returns an unsubscribe function.
- [menu/audio] sfx (src/audio/sfx.js): all 24 DESIGN 9 names are synthesized at runtime with WebAudio (no files). The AudioContext is created or resumed on the first pointerdown/keydown/touchend; earlier `play()` calls return false. Positional sounds: full volume within 8 tiles of the listener, falling to silence at 30 tiles, panned up to ±0.75 by horizontal offset; without `setListener` (or with NaN) every sound plays as non-positional. UI sounds (click, select, error, victory, defeat, tier_up, horn) ignore distance. Per-name rate limits (minimum gap + max simultaneous voices, e.g. 5 arrows, 6 hits) plus a 40-voice cap that UI sounds bypass; overlapping copies of one name are attenuated so a 50-archer volley is not louder than a few arrows. Master volume follows a v^1.5 curve. Extras: `SFX_NAMES`, `distanceGain(d)`, `sfx.unlock()`, `sfx.ready`, `sfx.init({ context, volume })` (inject a context in tests), `renderSfxOffline(name)` (OfflineAudioContext render, browser only). In Node everything is a no-op.
- [menu/audio] main.js is provisional: `startGame(launch)` stores the launch in `globalThis.__mythicwarLaunch` (debug/test hook), hides the menu and shows a "Game starting… (game view not wired yet)" placeholder with Back to Menu. boot() wires the saved volume into sfx and live volume changes. The integration owner replaces the placeholder body and keeps the rest.
- [menu/audio] style.css scopes all menu styles under `.mw-root` (the menu sizes in em from `font-size: clamp(13px, 1.25vmin + 5px, 40px)` on `.mw-root`, not on html, so the HUD's rem is untouched), plus page basics: html/body full-size black, `#app` fixed, `#game-root` absolute inset 0, `[hidden] { display: none !important }`. Responsive: one column under 1150px, two-row slot rows under 760px, crest/tagline hidden under 640px height; every screen scrolls when taller than the window.
- [menu/audio] Menu backdrop (src/ui/menu-fx.js) is four stacked canvases (sky and mountains painted per resize; stars and embers animated ≤ 45 fps, ≤ 30 fps with half the embers behind non-title screens). It measures the frame rate it actually gets and steps down by itself (to 20 fps, then to a still picture) when the browser delivers under 14 fps for 2 s, which keeps the menu usable under software rendering and on weak GPUs at 4K. Screen transitions animate transform only, so content is readable even if no animation frame is ever rendered. prefers-reduced-motion gives a still sky.
- [menu/audio] Correction to the sfx entry above: distance and panning apply to any sound played with `{ x, y }` while a listener is set; a sound played without a position is non-positional. The `ui` flag (click, select, move, error, building_complete, train, summon_god, summon_titan, victory, defeat, tier_up, horn) only lets a sound bypass the 40-voice cap.

- [simfix] Gold economy now runs at a WC3 pace. Constants (src/config.js): `MINE_TIME` 2.0 → **1.5 s** per 10-gold trip; new `MINE_SLOTS` = **2** workers mining one mine at once (shared by every owner); `CARRY_AMOUNT` stays 10. Each mine has 16 standing spots, one per tile around its 3×3 footprint and 0.42 tiles outside the edge. A worker claims the free passable spot nearest its owner's nearest drop-off (+1 % of its own distance as a tie-break) and keeps it for the whole gather order. Spots it cannot reach are skipped for the rest of that order. At its spot the worker waits (order phase 'wait', state 'gather', standing still, ghosted) in a per-mine FIFO queue (`mine.waiting`). A freed slot goes to the longest waiter at once (`handOff`), and a worker that leaves, dies or is pushed away frees its slot lazily (validity is checked like prayer slots). - [simfix] Melee surround (src/sim/surround.js, used by `engage` for ground melee attackers within `SURROUND_DIST` = 7 of the target's edge). Around a unit target the attacker picks a spot on a ring of radius rT + rU + 0.25 (gap = min(0.25, range/2)). Units standing there (not moving, or attacking) and other attackers' claims (`target.claimers`, validated lazily) block arcs. The candidates are straight ahead plus every blocked arc's edge, nearest in angle first. When terrain or a bad-spot mark rejected a candidate the ring is also sampled, so a target hugging a building is still reachable. Around a building the spots lie on a rounded rectangle at rU + 0.25 from the footprint, nearest first. The attacker steers directly to its spot, walking around the ring (waypoints 0.6 rad ahead on an outer ring) instead of pushing through the crowd, and follows a path when terrain is in the way. It re-checks its spot every 12 ticks, keeps it while it is free, and marks a spot bad for 3 s after 10 ticks without 0.15 tiles of progress. An attacker that is hitting holds its claim where it stands. With no free spot it waits outside the crowd (edge distance gap + 4 rU + 0.4, stepping back out of the lane) and counts as blocked. Measured with 20 warriors: attack-move into 4 spread enemies, 20/20 hitting by 6 s (before, 17–18 with units overlapping); into 4 clumped enemies, 19/20 by 8 s spread over ≥ 3 targets; an explicit attack on one warrior fills a ring of 9 while the rest wait clear; a barracks gets 20/20 by 7 s. - [netfix] Liveness (src/net/transport.js): a connection's silence is counted in time the page actually ran, not wall-clock time.
